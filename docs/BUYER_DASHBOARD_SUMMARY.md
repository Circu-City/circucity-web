## 🔄 Order Status Flow

```
Customer Places Order
    ↓
PENDING (awaiting payment)
    ↓
PAID (payment confirmed) ← Can cancel here
    ↓
SHIPPED (on the way)
    ↓
DELIVERED (completed)

Cancellation Path:
PENDING/PAID → CANCELLED → Refund Processed
```

## 🎨 Status Colors

- 🟡 **PENDING** - Yellow
- 🟢 **PAID** - Green
- 🔵 **SHIPPED** - Blue
- 🟢 **DELIVERED** - Green
- 🔴 **CANCELLED** - Red
- 🟣 **REFUNDED** - Purple

## 📋 Business Rules

### Can Cancel Order:
✅ Status is PENDING or PAID
✅ User owns the order
✅ Not yet shipped

### Cannot Cancel Order:
❌ Already SHIPPED or DELIVERED
❌ Already CANCELLED or REFUNDED

### Refund Processing:
- Automatic for PAID orders
- Full refund to original payment method
- 5-10 business days
- Inventory automatically restored
